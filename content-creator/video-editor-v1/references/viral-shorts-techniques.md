# Viral Short-Form Video Techniques for Talking-Head Creators

Research date: 2026-02-10.
Scope: TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts retention technique for a one-person selfie-camera talking-head video of roughly 1-3 minutes, aimed at a Thai personal-brand creator.
Method: web research against platform official docs (TikTok Ads creative best practices, YouTube blog), editor/creator breakdowns (Alex Hormozi, Ali Abdaal, MrBeast leaked production guide), tool-vendor studies with data (OpusClip, KreateFlo, BlitzCut), and Thai marketing sources.
Numbers are quoted from the cited sources; treat vendor "internal data" claims as directional, not peer-reviewed.

## 1. The Hook: First 1-3 Seconds

### The window, with numbers

- Viewers decide to stay or swipe within the first 2-3 seconds of a vertical video (https://toneproduction.net/short-form-video-hook/).
- TikTok's Creative Center treats the first 2 seconds as the make-or-break moment, and TikTok's ad docs say 50% of total ad recall impact happens in the first 2 seconds (https://www.admove.ai/blog/social-media-hooks-guide, https://coinis.com/how-to/write-tiktok-ad-hook).
- TikTok's official creative guidance: hook in the first 6 seconds, and introduce the content proposition within the first 3 seconds for better recall (https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/creative-best-practices).
- TikTok's business blog states 90% of ad recall impact is captured within the first 6 seconds (https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/creative-best-practices-top-performing-ads).
- Over 70% of TikTok users decide whether to stay or scroll within the first 3 seconds (https://www.admove.ai/blog/social-media-hooks-guide).
- Videos that retain at least 60% of viewers past the 3-second mark are significantly more likely to be pushed to the For You page, Reels feed, or Shorts shelf (https://toneproduction.net/short-form-video-hook/).
- TikTok data shows videos with an effective opening earn up to 2x the engagement and watch-through rates (https://toneproduction.net/short-form-video-hook/).
- The completion-rate threshold for viral distribution on TikTok has climbed to roughly 70%, up from around 50% in 2024 (https://www.admove.ai/blog/social-media-hooks-guide).
- An analysis of viral TikToks found 84.3% used psychological hook triggers within the first 3 seconds (https://www.admove.ai/blog/social-media-hooks-guide citing TTS Vibes).
- Agency data: the right pattern interrupt can raise watch time up to 85% and conversion up to 32%, and Facebook reports 3x higher engagement for videos with pattern interrupts in the first 3 seconds (https://inceptly.com/simple-video-tricks-that-stop-the-scroll/).
- TikTok internal research: pattern-based attention hooks increase completion rates by 41% (https://inceptly.com/simple-video-tricks-that-stop-the-scroll/).
- MrBeast's leaked production guide: "the first minute has the most loss" on almost every YouTube video, so front-load the best material and obsess over the opening (https://simonwillison.net/2024/Sep/15/how-to-succeed-in-mrbeast-production/, PDF: https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6623b7720b009050313e701c/66ede69453b7bbadcd2f05a8_How-To-Succeed-At-MrBeast-Production%20(2).pdf).

### Hook types that work for talking-head videos

- Five structures that consistently work: the Contrarian Claim ("everything you've been told about X is wrong"), the Curiosity Gap (hint without revealing), the Mistake Warning (name an error the viewer is making right now), the Payoff-First Open (show the result, then explain), and the In-Media-Res Story Open (start mid-story with no setup) (https://toneproduction.net/short-form-video-hook/).
- Seven hook types with their psychology: Curiosity, Story (open loop), Problem/Pain (loss aversion), Controversial (pattern interrupt), List/Number (specificity bias), Value/Benefit (reward anticipation), Question (https://www.admove.ai/blog/social-media-hooks-guide).
- Negative framing beats positive framing in most A/B tests because of loss aversion: "5 mistakes killing your growth" pulls harder than "5 ways to grow" (https://www.admove.ai/blog/social-media-hooks-guide).
- Thai marketing sources teach the same taxonomy: visual hook (moving or unusual first shot), text hook (big on-screen headline like "หยุดทำแบบนี้ถ้าไม่อยากอ้วน"), and audio hook (trending sound or a question that "แทงใจดำ") (https://wantalkmarketing.com/marketing/video-retention/).
- Thai creator source: 7 techniques for opening clips by stopping the thumb in the first 3 seconds, including opening with a question (https://www.facebook.com/MarketThinkTH/posts/1261356722705805, https://www.tiktok.com/@digitalshortcut/video/7350615819581246721).
- Every hook must have immediacy (lands under 3 seconds), truthfulness (the video must deliver the promise), and specificity (a number, a result, or a precise frustration) (https://www.admove.ai/blog/social-media-hooks-guide, https://toneproduction.net/short-form-video-hook/).

### On-screen text hook

- Keep the first on-screen text element to 7 words or fewer, positioned in the safe zone away from platform UI (https://toneproduction.net/short-form-video-hook/).
- A written hook title should stay on screen for the first 3-5 seconds (https://www.tiktok.com/@ricoincarnati/video/7686953343310187789).
- The text overlay should escalate or complement the spoken hook, not duplicate it word for word, so silent viewers get a parallel reason to stay (https://toneproduction.net/short-form-video-hook/).
- Spoken hooks should land within the first 3 seconds, which is roughly 10-14 words (https://toneproduction.net/short-form-video-hook/).
- Never open with a greeting or self-introduction: phrases like "Hey guys, welcome back" are "the equivalent of clearing your throat" (https://toneproduction.net/short-form-video-hook/).
- TikTok's own ads guidance recommends using captions or text overlays to provide context, since many users watch in sound-sensitive environments (https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/creative-best-practices).

### Visual pattern interrupt in the hook

- Fire a visual jolt within the first 1.5 seconds, ideally at the 0.8-1.2 second mark, just as the viewer is deciding whether to keep watching (https://inceptly.com/simple-video-tricks-that-stop-the-scroll/).
- Effective visual jolts: an abrupt push-in zoom, a speed change, a jump cut already in frame one, or motion in the very first frame (https://inceptly.com/simple-video-tricks-that-stop-the-scroll/, https://www.admove.ai/blog/social-media-hooks-guide).
- Only 0.04% of 13.5 million analyzed short-form clips use a dedicated visual hook (kinetic text, zoom, flash), so it is a strong differentiator (https://www.opus.pro/research/broll-visual-effects-short-form).
- The hook is a pre-production job: storyboard the first frame deliberately and shoot 2-3 alternate openings, because editing cannot manufacture a pattern interrupt from a slow setup (https://toneproduction.net/short-form-video-hook/).

### Open loops

- An open loop starts a story and delays the resolution ("I lost 10K followers before I figured this out..."), which exploits the Zeigarnik effect: the brain cannot let an unfinished narrative go (https://www.admove.ai/blog/social-media-hooks-guide).
- A curiosity gap ("the one mistake killing your engagement") works the same way by creating a question the brain needs answered (https://www.admove.ai/blog/social-media-hooks-guide).
- Keep the gap answerable within the video's runtime; overpromising causes early drop-off when viewers realize the video cannot deliver, and the algorithm penalizes it (https://toneproduction.net/short-form-video-hook/).
- Run the "Mute Test" on your first 3 seconds: if the hook only works with audio, it is incomplete, because much of the feed is browsed muted (https://toneproduction.net/short-form-video-hook/).

## 2. Retention Editing

### Jump cuts and silence removal

- A 30-minute raw talking-head recording typically contains 4-7 minutes of pure dead space: silences, "um"s, false starts, and breath pauses (https://chatcut.io/blog/remove-silence-filler-words-from-video-2026).
- A 60-minute video tightened to 50 minutes by silence removal typically gains 12-18% higher completion rate (https://chatcut.io/blog/remove-silence-filler-words-from-video-2026).
- A 25-second vertical clip has no room for dead air at all; cut the source tight first (https://chatcut.io/blog/remove-silence-filler-words-from-video-2026).
- The Hormozi standard: aggressive jump cuts every 1-3 seconds between a medium shot and a tighter close-up, with zero dead air and every pause and filler word removed in post (https://www.choppity.com/tools/recreate-video-editing-style/alex-hormozi/).
- Cuts should land on emphasis words so the rhythm feels speech-driven rather than mechanical (https://www.choppity.com/tools/recreate-video-editing-style/alex-hormozi/).
- The jump-cut sweet spot for most creators is every 2-3 sentences; cutting after every single word fatigues viewers (https://edicionvideopro.com/en/editing-for-platforms-video-marketing/pattern-interrupts-tiktok-retention-guide/).
- Thai sources agree: "ความเงียบคือศัตรูของคลิปสั้น" - silence is the enemy, so cut breaths and "เอ่อ อ่า" completely and use jump cuts between sentences (https://wantalkmarketing.com/marketing/video-retention/).

### Pacing: how often to change something

- Videos with pattern interrupts every 3-5 seconds show 40-60% higher average retention, per an internal analysis of 200+ TikToks (https://edicionvideopro.com/en/editing-for-platforms-video-marketing/pattern-interrupts-tiktok-retention-guide/).
- For long-form, AIR's analysis of 100 channels found top-25% channels hold viewers 20-30% longer, and their "progressive rhythm" pattern changes something visually every 10-20 seconds early in the video; scale that down to every 3-5 seconds for vertical shorts (https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/advanced-retention-editing-cutting-patterns-that-keep-viewers-past-minute-8).
- Thai guidance says the same: if the same angle sits static for more than 5 seconds, boredom starts, so change angle, zoom, B-roll, or pop text to reset visual attention (https://wantalkmarketing.com/marketing/video-retention/).
- Alternating interrupt types matters: do not use the same device 5 times in a row, and pick 2-3 interrupt types per video instead of all 7, which reads as chaotic (https://edicionvideopro.com/en/editing-for-platforms-video-marketing/pattern-interrupts-tiktok-retention-guide/).

### Punch-in zooms

- Frequent 10-20% digital punch-ins on emphasis are the core of the Hormozi look, keeping visual variety from a single camera angle while the speaker fills at least 60% of the frame (https://www.choppity.com/tools/recreate-video-editing-style/alex-hormozi/).
- Zooms must be fast, about 0.3-0.5 seconds; slow 1-second zooms lose the interruption effect (https://edicionvideopro.com/en/editing-for-platforms-video-marketing/pattern-interrupts-tiktok-retention-guide/).
- Zoom on the important word, facial reaction, or object, and sync it with the audio beat or keyword (https://edicionvideopro.com/en/editing-for-platforms-video-marketing/pattern-interrupts-tiktok-retention-guide/).

### Mid-video re-hooks

- The "Narrative Loop" cutting pattern returns to the original hook or premise every 2-3 minutes with a visual or verbal callback; used by MrBeast and Yes Theory on long-form, and scaled to a mid-clip re-hook for shorts (https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/advanced-retention-editing-cutting-patterns-that-keep-viewers-past-minute-8).
- The "Contrast" pattern breaks calm talking-head pacing with a 5-10 cut burst sequence every 2-3 minutes, then returns to calm, which mimics natural conversation intensity (https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/advanced-retention-editing-cutting-patterns-that-keep-viewers-past-minute-8).
- Practical short-form rule: place your second-best moment around the 40-60% mark so a viewer who survives the hook hits another spike before deciding to leave.
- MrBeast structures retention in acts: hook at the start, "no dull moments" through the middle, re-engagement moments placed deliberately, and a satisfying payoff at the end with an abrupt ending (How to Succeed in MrBeast Production PDF, mirrored at https://simonwillison.net/2024/Sep/15/how-to-succeed-in-mrbeast-production/).

### Ending: loop and CTA

- Since March 31, 2025, YouTube counts every loop or replay of a Short as an additional view, so a seamless loop directly inflates views and distribution signals (https://virvid.ai/blog/looping-structure-shorts-retention-2026).
- Average Shorts retention is 73% versus 52% for long-form, and looping Shorts routinely push past 100% retention in YouTube Analytics (https://virvid.ai/blog/looping-structure-shorts-retention-2026).
- Loop anatomy that works: callback hook (the last line echoes the opening question), visual match cut (last frame matches the first), and audio continuity (music continues across the loop point with no silence gap) (https://virvid.ai/blog/looping-structure-shorts-retention-2026).
- End "mid-energy, not mid-sentence": never freeze the final frame or fade to black, because a hard stop or static frame tells the viewer it is over (https://virvid.ai/blog/looping-structure-shorts-retention-2026).
- Shorts over 35 seconds rarely loop effectively because the narrative callback weakens, so treat looping as a bonus rather than the main plan for a 2-3 minute video (https://virvid.ai/blog/looping-structure-shorts-retention-2026).
- Place the CTA at the 70-85% mark of the video, after value is delivered; a CTA in the first few seconds rarely works (https://store.hollyland.com/blogs/creator-hub/tiktok-ads-best-practices).
- Do not make the CTA sound like the end of the video, because viewers leave the moment they sense "the end" (https://www.facebook.com/UpperhandCreative/posts/890205859777258/).
- A closing line like "และนั่นคือเหตุผลที่..." that loops back to the opening sentence is the Thai-creator description of the seamless-loop trick (https://wantalkmarketing.com/marketing/video-retention/).

## 3. Captions

### The numbers on why captions matter

- Videos with captions see 12% to 40% higher average watch time depending on niche (https://www.opus.pro/blog/tiktok-caption-subtitle-best-practices).
- 80% of people say they are more likely to finish a video with captions, and 69% watch with sound off in public (Verizon Media / Publicis Media survey of 5,616 US adults, 2019, summarized at https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-fonts-tiktok).
- On-screen captions in TikTok ads produced +58% recall and +31% likeability (TikTok Marketing Science US SMB Creative Effectiveness Study 2021, via https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-fonts-tiktok).
- Captions increased Facebook video view time by 12% in Facebook's internal testing (via https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-fonts-tiktok).
- About 85% of social feed videos are watched muted, which makes captions the primary visual engine of mobile watch time (https://kreateflo.com/blog/which-animated-caption-styles-actually-increase-video-watch-time).
- Note the platform contrast: roughly 95% of TikTok videos are watched with sound on, so audio hooks work there, but captions are still required for muted replay contexts (https://inceptly.com/simple-video-tricks-that-stop-the-scroll/).

### Style that performs

- The winning look: bold sans-serif, ALL CAPS or large title case, white text with a thick black stroke, word-by-word pop-in, and the currently spoken word highlighted in yellow (https://www.choppity.com/tools/recreate-video-editing-style/alex-hormozi/).
- Chunk captions into 3 to 7 word segments displayed 1 to 3 seconds each, broken at natural speech pauses (https://www.opus.pro/blog/tiktok-caption-subtitle-best-practices).
- For clips over 30 seconds, switch from single-word karaoke to 2-3 word chunks at 300-500 ms per chunk to prevent eye strain; reserve single-word pops (150-250 ms per word) for clips under 30 seconds (https://kreateflo.com/blog/which-animated-caption-styles-actually-increase-video-watch-time).
- Word-by-word captions generate 60 to 120 micro-visual attention resets per 30-second clip versus only 5 to 10 for static blocks (https://kreateflo.com/blog/which-animated-caption-styles-actually-increase-video-watch-time).
- Reveal each word 100-200 milliseconds before the audio hits; reading is faster than listening, so exact sync feels laggy (https://kreateflo.com/blog/which-animated-caption-styles-actually-increase-video-watch-time).
- Hormozi-style single-word reveals boost relative completion rates by 50-80% over uncaptioned footage, and properly styled dynamic captions raise overall watch time by 23-40% (https://kreateflo.com/blog/which-animated-caption-styles-actually-increase-video-watch-time).
- Limit color highlights to 1-2 keywords per phrase: keep about 80% of text white with a black outline, and use yellow #FFD93D or electric green #39FF14 only on focal words, because highlighting everything destroys hierarchy (https://kreateflo.com/blog/which-animated-caption-styles-actually-increase-video-watch-time).
- Make numbers a different color and slightly larger, since the eye locks onto high-contrast elements and this is an attention-guiding trick (https://www.opus.pro/blog/tiktok-caption-subtitle-best-practices).
- Emoji are part of the classic Hormozi look, but the 2026 "dynamic minimalism" trend strips flashing emojis and neon in favor of clean sans-serif with keyword highlights in brand colors; keep emoji occasional if your brand is not "hype" (https://joyspace.ai/hormozi-editing-style-2026-analysis).

### Font and size

- Most talking-head creators use Montserrat Bold or Montserrat Black, with Poppins, Bebas Neue, Anton (free Hormozi substitute), and TikTok Sans as common alternatives (https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-fonts-tiktok).
- Caption font size of 60-75px at 1080x1920 fits most talking-head content, with the whole caption block at 12-20% of frame height (https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-fonts-tiktok).
- The KreateFlo Hormozi preset: Montserrat Black, 85pt equivalent, ALL CAPS, 12px black stroke (https://kreateflo.com/blog/which-animated-caption-styles-actually-increase-video-watch-time).
- General subtitle guidance is 1/20 to 1/10 of frame height, which for 1920px tall vertical video means roughly 96-190px for full-size caption text; stay near the lower end for 2-3 line blocks (https://vsubtitle.com/subtitle-font-size-and-reading-speed-2026/).
- Show 2-4 words visible at a time for word-by-word styles (https://www.choppity.com/tools/recreate-video-editing-style/alex-hormozi/).

### Position and safe zones at 1080x1920

- TikTok: keep text 130px below the top edge, clear the bottom 250-350px (caption text, sound bar, engagement UI), and leave 60px on the left plus 120-164px on the right for the like/comment/share column; usable area is roughly 960x1386px centered (https://creators.ramd.am/creator-school/understanding-the-safe-zone, https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-placement-short-form-video).
- Instagram Reels: keep text 108-220px below the top, clear the bottom 310-450px (expanded in late 2025), and leave about 120px on the right; usable area is roughly 900x1440px centered (https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-placement-short-form-video).
- YouTube Shorts: keep text about 120px below the top, clear the bottom ~300px and right ~48px; usable area is roughly 984x1500px centered, the smallest UI penalty of the three (https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-placement-short-form-video).
- Cross-platform universal safe zone: keep all caption text inside a 900x1400px centered rectangle in the 1080x1920 frame (https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-placement-short-form-video).
- Put captions in the lower-middle third of the safe zone: start the block around Y=1200-1300px from the top and end no lower than Y=1550 (370px from bottom), which keeps text near the speaker's face and reduces eye travel (https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-placement-short-form-video).
- Alternative official-ish guidance puts the safest caption zone at center-middle to upper-middle, avoiding the bottom 25% and top 15% of the frame (https://www.opus.pro/blog/tiktok-caption-subtitle-best-practices).
- The most commonly missed mistake is the right-edge dead zone: centered long lines have their last characters hidden behind TikTok's 120-164px button column (https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-placement-short-form-video).
- Thai source collecting the same safe-zone numbers for Reels, TikTok, and Shorts exists for local reference (https://www.facebook.com/buddyreview/posts/1476621407814467).

## 4. B-Roll, Images, Memes, Screenshots, Icons

### How much and how long

- B-roll appears in 6.0% of 13.5 million analyzed short-form clips and is the most widely used visual enhancement beyond captions (https://www.opus.pro/research/broll-visual-effects-short-form).
- The most performant podcast-style clips include B-roll covering 15-35% of total clip time (https://www.conbersa.ai/learn/podcast-clip-b-roll-overlays).
- Purposeful B-roll lifts retention 5-20%; B-roll used as filler with footage disconnected from the dialogue reduces retention 5-15% (https://www.conbersa.ai/learn/podcast-clip-b-roll-overlays).
- Full-frame B-roll shots should run 2-8 seconds while the voice continues; a 10-second B-roll cut breaks the parasocial connection with the speaker (https://www.conbersa.ai/learn/podcast-clip-b-roll-overlays).
- B-roll helps when it directly visualizes what the speaker says (product, place, process, statistic as a graphic) and hurts when it is generic stock that does not match the words (https://www.conbersa.ai/learn/podcast-clip-b-roll-overlays).

### Full-screen vs split vs picture-in-picture

- Four overlay formats: full-frame overlay (replaces the speaker for 2-8 seconds), picture-in-picture (small window over the speaker so the face stays visible), split-screen (speaker and visual side by side, common for demos and comparisons), and graphic overlay (charts, stats, text emphasis on top of the speaker) (https://www.conbersa.ai/learn/podcast-clip-b-roll-overlays).
- For personal-brand talking-head clips, graphic overlays and short full-frame cutaways preserve the face connection better than long full-frame replacements.
- Text overlays appear in 4.5% of analyzed clips and should be used for key statistics, questions, step numbers, and CTAs, positioned so they do not compete with captions (https://www.opus.pro/research/broll-visual-effects-short-form).
- Intros in short-form should stay under 2 seconds or be skipped entirely; long intros cause immediate drop-off (https://www.opus.pro/research/broll-visual-effects-short-form).

## 5. Kinetic Typography: When to Use Big Keyword Pops

- Kinetic typography works through dual coding: the brain processes on-screen text and audio through different channels, and matching them increases comprehension and retention (https://joyspace.ai/hormozi-editing-style-2026-analysis).
- The rapid text motion also acts as "artificial pacing": even a slow speaker feels high-energy because the text moves fast, a constant stream of pattern interrupts (https://joyspace.ai/hormozi-editing-style-2026-analysis).
- Use big single-phrase pops for the hook, numbers, step labels ("ข้อ 1", "MISTAKE #1"), and the payoff line; use normal flowing captions for the connective talk (https://edicionvideopro.com/en/editing-for-platforms-video-marketing/pattern-interrupts-tiktok-retention-guide/).
- Pop-up text spec: scale from 0 to 100% in 0.2 seconds, bold sans-serif (Montserrat, Poppins, Impact), high-contrast color, on screen 1-2 seconds (https://edicionvideopro.com/en/editing-for-platforms-video-marketing/pattern-interrupts-tiktok-retention-guide/).
- Reserve the biggest type for one idea at a time; a wall of competing big text reads as chaos and the viewer reads nothing (https://edicionvideopro.com/en/editing-for-platforms-video-marketing/pattern-interrupts-tiktok-retention-guide/).
- For education-heavy talking-head content, a softer "kinetic slide" (2-3 word chunks with a 4-frame ease) raises watch time 12-25% without the jarring pop (https://kreateflo.com/blog/which-animated-caption-styles-actually-increase-video-watch-time).
- Match caption energy to clip length: bold kinetic pops for 15-60s clips, 2-3 word chunks with subtle bounce for 1-5 minutes, minimal lower-third sentences for 5+ minutes (https://kreateflo.com/blog/which-animated-caption-styles-actually-increase-video-watch-time).

## 6. Sound Design

### BGM level relative to voice

- The golden rule: the voice should be 15-25 dB louder than the background music (https://www.dl-sounds.com/how-to-mix-voice-and-music-in-videos/).
- Concrete starting levels for a social media reel: voice around -3 dBFS peak, music bed around -18 dBFS, with tutorials and podcast clips wanting music even lower at -22 to -28 dBFS (https://www.dl-sounds.com/how-to-mix-voice-and-music-in-videos/).
- Best music genres under speech: ambient, piano, acoustic, and "corporate" beds with weak melodies; avoid vocal-heavy or busy tracks that compete with the voice (https://www.dl-sounds.com/how-to-mix-voice-and-music-in-videos/).
- Run the 10-second test: play the mix at low volume, and if you cannot understand every word effortlessly, the music is too loud (https://www.dl-sounds.com/how-to-mix-voice-and-music-in-videos/).
- Duck music under speech by about 10-15 dB below its bed level and let it return between sentences; duck to a level, not to silence (https://store.hollyland.com/blogs/creator-hub/what-is-audio-ducking).
- The Hormozi look uses only subtle low-energy background music and no transition SFX, letting the voice carry the energy; other editors use SFX as "auditory bullet points" that mark idea transitions (https://www.choppity.com/tools/recreate-video-editing-style/alex-hormozi/, https://joyspace.ai/hormozi-editing-style-2026-analysis).

### Master loudness targets

- YouTube normalizes regular video playback to about -14 LUFS, which is why -14 LUFS integrated is the standard social-video target (https://veniamastering.studio/blog/how-loud-should-your-master-be-in-2026/, https://www.sageaudio.com/articles/best-mastering-level-for-streaming).
- A conservative production convention for TikTok/Reels delivery is -16 LUFS integrated with a -1 dBTP true-peak ceiling, since the platforms transcode audio and do not publish a universal numeric requirement (https://apu.software/tiktok-instagram-reels-loudness/).
- Practical recipe for a talking-head short: voice normalized to around -14 to -16 LUFS integrated for the full program, true peak at or below -1 dBTP, music ducked 10-15 dB under speech (https://apu.software/tiktok-instagram-reels-loudness/, https://store.hollyland.com/blogs/creator-hub/what-is-audio-ducking).

### SFX placement

- The three workhorse SFX families are whooshes (movement, transitions), risers (building anticipation into a reveal), and impacts/bass hits (landing a big statement or text pop) (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JOm_EqBoW0M).
- Sync each SFX to the exact frame of the cut or zoom it reinforces; audio plus visual together has double impact (https://edicionvideopro.com/en/editing-for-platforms-video-marketing/pattern-interrupts-tiktok-retention-guide/).
- Typical placements: whoosh on punch-ins and scene changes, pop or ding on each caption chunk or keyword highlight, riser into the payoff, bass hit on the hook line and the CTA (https://edicionvideopro.com/en/editing-for-platforms-video-marketing/pattern-interrupts-tiktok-retention-guide/, https://www.instagram.com/reel/DCMFQ0hJBP2/).
- Keep SFX at a lower level than the voice (start around -12 to -18 dBFS) so they accent rather than cover speech, and avoid triggering one on every single cut in a 2-3 minute video or they become noise (https://edicionvideopro.com/en/editing-for-platforms-video-marketing/pattern-interrupts-tiktok-retention-guide/).
- MrBeast's editing style depends on catchy music tracks and SFX placed to add adrenaline and suspense at specific video parts rather than being constant wallpaper (https://increditors.com/an-ultimate-guide-to-alex-hormozi-ali-abdaal-and-mr-beast-video-editing-style-and-methods/).

## 7. Length and Structure Templates

### Length data by platform

- TikTok's own 2021 data put the highest-performing videos at 21-34 seconds, but by mid-2024 half of watch time went to videos over one minute, and videos of 3-10 minutes received roughly double the views of 6-10 second videos (https://www.shortimize.com/blog/video-length-sweet-spots-tiktok-reels-shorts).
- For substantive talking-head content on TikTok, aim for 60-180 seconds; ultra-short still wins for trend-driven comedy (https://www.shortimize.com/blog/video-length-sweet-spots-tiktok-reels-shorts).
- Reels between 60 and 90 seconds achieve the highest average views and engagement, while 15-30 seconds maximizes reach through replays (https://www.shortimize.com/blog/video-length-sweet-spots-tiktok-reels-shorts).
- YouTube Shorts data still favors roughly 15-30 seconds, with average Shorts retention at 73% versus 52% for long-form (https://www.shortimize.com/blog/video-length-sweet-spots-tiktok-reels-shorts, https://virvid.ai/blog/looping-structure-shorts-retention-2026).
- A 2-3 minute talking-head clip sits in TikTok's rewarded "substantive" zone, needs a strong mid-video re-hook, and is above the length where looping reliably happens (https://www.shortimize.com/blog/video-length-sweet-spots-tiktok-reels-shorts, https://virvid.ai/blog/looping-structure-shorts-retention-2026).
- YouTube's official creator blog: Shorts are capped at 3 minutes, so get the message across quickly and capture attention in the first few seconds (https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/your-guide-to-getting-started-with-youtube-shorts/).

### Structure templates

- Hook-Value-Action: hook stops the scroll in 1-3 seconds, value delivers exactly what the hook promised (a bait-and-switch kills retention fastest of all), action tells the viewer what to do next (https://www.admove.ai/blog/social-media-hooks-guide).
- Problem-Agitate-Solution (PAS): name a specific problem, agitate with the exact moment the viewer gets stuck, then deliver the solution; agitation works best when specific, not abstract (https://www.storyprompt.com/blog/problem-agitate-solution-the-pas-storytelling-framework, https://shortgenius.com/blog/script-writing-techniques).
- Thai three-part story structure: เปิด (what is the problem - the hook), กลาง (the value or how-to), จบ (payoff or result), with the rule "don't reveal the answer too early - feed curiosity until the last seconds" (https://wantalkmarketing.com/marketing/video-retention/).
- List structure: a numbered promise ("5 things I wish I knew") lowers commitment by telling the brain exactly what to expect, and each number is a natural kinetic-typography pop and re-hook (https://www.admove.ai/blog/social-media-hooks-guide, https://edicionvideopro.com/en/editing-for-platforms-video-marketing/pattern-interrupts-tiktok-retention-guide/).
- MrBeast's format principle: pick formats with a built-in payoff at the end (viewers get invested in progress and want to see the outcome), and stair-step escalating reveals to keep momentum (How to Succeed in MrBeast Production PDF via https://simonwillison.net/2024/Sep/15/how-to-succeed-in-mrbeast-production/).

### How to end

- Satisfying payoff followed by an abrupt ending beats a long outro; MrBeast explicitly coaches "satisfying payoff ... with an abrupt ending" (How to Succeed in MrBeast Production PDF).
- Loop endings work best under 35 seconds; for longer clips, end mid-energy on the payoff and put the CTA at 70-85% instead of the last second (https://virvid.ai/blog/looping-structure-shorts-retention-2026, https://store.hollyland.com/blogs/creator-hub/tiktok-ads-best-practices).
- Loop-breaking mistakes: a hard stop, a static freeze frame, an over-explained ending ("thanks for watching, follow for more"), and background music with a clear final note at the loop point (https://virvid.ai/blog/looping-structure-shorts-retention-2026).
- A cliffhanger or open-question close ("the answer is actually in the first second...") drives rewatches and comments (https://virvid.ai/blog/looping-structure-shorts-retention-2026).

## 8. Common Mistakes That Kill Retention

- Wasting the first 3 seconds on greetings, logos, or self-introduction; every source lists this first (https://shortzly.com/blog/short-form-video-mistakes-to-avoid, https://toneproduction.net/short-form-video-hook/).
- Long pauses between sentences and slow pacing that takes too long to reach the point (https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nkembohisaiah-video-editor_most-creators-think-bad-content-kills-retention-activity-7437750630275784704-aPia).
- A "false start": opening with content that is not the video's real subject, which breaks the hook promise (https://georgeblackman.substack.com/p/5-retention-killing-youtube-mistakes).
- "Too much too soon" and "overwhelm": cramming every technique and every idea into the opening instead of pacing one idea at a time (https://georgeblackman.substack.com/p/5-retention-killing-youtube-mistakes).
- Skipping captions or adding them sloppily, which forfeits the muted-scroll audience (https://shortzly.com/blog/short-form-video-mistakes-to-avoid).
- Static multi-line subtitle blocks; viewers scan the block in half a second and swipe while waiting for the audio (https://kreateflo.com/blog/which-animated-caption-styles-actually-increase-video-watch-time).
- Over-highlighting captions: coloring every word destroys the visual hierarchy that makes keyword highlights work (https://kreateflo.com/blog/which-animated-caption-styles-actually-increase-video-watch-time).
- Captions outside the safe zone, hidden behind the like button, username row, or caption text (https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-placement-short-form-video).
- Generic stock B-roll disconnected from the dialogue; it reads as an ad and cuts retention 5-15% (https://www.conbersa.ai/learn/podcast-clip-b-roll-overlays).
- B-roll or cutaways that overstay past about 10 seconds and break the face connection (https://www.conbersa.ai/learn/podcast-clip-b-roll-overlays).
- Music mixed too loud over the voice, the fastest way to make viewers leave (https://www.dl-sounds.com/how-to-mix-voice-and-music-in-videos/).
- Using every pattern-interrupt type at once, which feels chaotic and amateur; pick 2-3 and stay consistent (https://edicionvideopro.com/en/editing-for-platforms-video-marketing/pattern-interrupts-tiktok-retention-guide/).
- Hook-content mismatch: a strong hook followed by weak delivery tanks completion and future trust (https://www.admove.ai/blog/social-media-hooks-guide, https://toneproduction.net/short-form-video-hook/).
- Repeating the same hook formula every post; hook fatigue trains the audience to skip your opener, so rotate 5-10 structures (https://toneproduction.net/short-form-video-hook/).
- A long outro, freeze-frame ending, or CTA that announces "the video is over", all of which cut the session short (https://virvid.ai/blog/looping-structure-shorts-retention-2026).
- A static frame lasting more than about 5 seconds anywhere in the clip (Thai guidance: แช่ภาพนิ่งเกิน 5 วินาที คนดูเริ่มเบื่อ) (https://wantalkmarketing.com/marketing/video-retention/).

## Actionable checklist for editing a 2:48 Thai talking-head clip

At 168 seconds, the clip sits in TikTok's rewarded 60-180 second "substantive" zone, so it needs a 3-beat structure, a mid-clip re-hook, captions engineered for mute viewing, and disciplined audio.

- Hook line: write the first spoken sentence to land a specific promise or contrarian claim within the first 3 seconds, roughly 10-14 Thai words, no greeting and no self-introduction (https://toneproduction.net/short-form-video-hook/).
- Put a 5-7 word Thai text hook on screen from frame 0 to second 4-5, in safe zone, styled differently from the captions (https://toneproduction.net/short-form-video-hook/, https://www.tiktok.com/@ricoincarnati/video/7686953343310187789).
- Fire a 10-20% punch-in zoom within the first 1.5 seconds, animated in about 0.3-0.5 seconds, with a whoosh at the same frame (https://inceptly.com/simple-video-tricks-that-stop-the-scroll/, https://edicionvideopro.com/en/editing-for-platforms-video-marketing/pattern-interrupts-tiktok-retention-guide/).
- Open a loop in the hook and pay it off only in the final 20 seconds, so viewers must watch through (https://www.admove.ai/blog/social-media-hooks-guide).
- Remove every silence, breath, "เอ่อ/อ่า", and dead pause; target a cut or visual change every 3-5 seconds across the whole 168 seconds (https://chatcut.io/blog/remove-silence-filler-words-from-video-2026, https://edicionvideopro.com/en/editing-for-platforms-video-marketing/pattern-interrupts-tiktok-retention-guide/).
- Alternate jump cuts every 2-3 sentences with 10-20% punch-ins on emphasis words; never zoom slower than 0.5 seconds (https://www.choppity.com/tools/recreate-video-editing-style/alex-hormozi/).
- Insert your second-best moment (proof, number, screenshot, or demo) around 1:10-1:40 as a re-hook spike at the 40-60% mark (https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/advanced-retention-editing-cutting-patterns-that-keep-viewers-past-minute-8).
- Add B-roll, screenshots, or memes covering 15-35% of runtime, each shot 2-8 seconds full-frame or as a corner PiP while your face stays visible (https://www.conbersa.ai/learn/podcast-clip-b-roll-overlays).
- Every B-roll must match a spoken word or claim; if it is generic decoration, cut it (https://www.conbersa.ai/learn/podcast-clip-b-roll-overlays).
- Captions: 2-3 Thai words per chunk (or word-by-word on the 3-4 highest-energy lines), each chunk visible 0.3-0.5 seconds, revealed 100-200 ms ahead of the voice (https://kreateflo.com/blog/which-animated-caption-styles-actually-increase-video-watch-time).
- Caption look: bold sans-serif (Montserrat Bold/Black or a Thai equivalent like IBM Plex Sans Thai Bold or Prompt Bold), white fill, 12px-equivalent black stroke, one keyword per phrase in yellow #FFD93D or brand color, about 80% of words left plain (https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-fonts-tiktok, https://kreateflo.com/blog/which-animated-caption-styles-actually-increase-video-watch-time).
- Caption size: 60-75px at 1080x1920, block width under 900px, kept inside the universal safe rectangle (https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-fonts-tiktok, https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-placement-short-form-video).
- Caption position: center the block between Y=1200 and Y=1550 in the 1080x1920 frame; keep 130px top, 320px bottom, and 140px right margins on TikTok (https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-placement-short-form-video).
- Big keyword pops: reserve 3-5 full-screen text moments for the hook, the list numbers ("ข้อ 1/2/3"), and the payoff; each pop scales 0-100% in 0.2 seconds and holds 1-2 seconds (https://edicionvideopro.com/en/editing-for-platforms-video-marketing/pattern-interrupts-tiktok-retention-guide/).
- Make every number a different color and slightly larger than the surrounding caption (https://www.opus.pro/blog/tiktok-caption-subtitle-best-practices).
- Audio: voice peaks around -3 dBFS and carries the program loudness at -14 to -16 LUFS integrated with a -1 dBTP ceiling (https://www.dl-sounds.com/how-to-mix-voice-and-music-in-videos/, https://apu.software/tiktok-instagram-reels-loudness/).
- Music: one low-energy instrumental bed at 15-25 dB below the voice (about -18 dBFS), ducked a further 10-15 dB whenever you speak, returning in pauses (https://www.dl-sounds.com/how-to-mix-voice-and-music-in-videos/, https://store.hollyland.com/blogs/creator-hub/what-is-audio-ducking).
- SFX budget: whoosh on the opening punch-in and 2-3 major scene changes, pop or ding on each big keyword pop, one riser into the payoff, one bass hit on the CTA; never SFX every cut (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JOm_EqBoW0M, https://edicionvideopro.com/en/editing-for-platforms-video-marketing/pattern-interrupts-tiktok-retention-guide/).
- End with the payoff and an abrupt stop, mid-energy, no freeze frame, no fade to black, no "ฝากกดติดตามด้วยนะ" as the last line (https://virvid.ai/blog/looping-structure-shorts-retention-2026).
- If the ending can echo the opening line, cut it so the clip loops back seamlessly; bonus views on Shorts and rewatches on TikTok (https://virvid.ai/blog/looping-structure-shorts-retention-2026, https://wantalkmarketing.com/marketing/video-retention/).
- Place the follow CTA at 70-85% (around 2:10-2:20), spoken over the payoff, phrased so the video keeps moving ("ติดตามไว้ สัปดาห์หน้าเจาะวิธีทำจริง") (https://store.hollyland.com/blogs/creator-hub/tiktok-ads-best-practices).
- QC pass 1: watch muted; the story, captions, and text hooks must carry the video alone (https://toneproduction.net/short-form-video-hook/).
- QC pass 2: watch at phone size and check no caption enters the 130/320/140px TikTok dead zones on any platform (https://blitzcutai.com/blog/best-caption-placement-short-form-video).
- Check analytics after posting: 3-second view rate above 30% (aim 70%+ viewed-vs-swiped on Shorts), completion trending toward 70%, and fix the hook first if they miss (https://toneproduction.net/short-form-video-hook/, https://www.admove.ai/blog/social-media-hooks-guide).
