Rules for the plan:

- Hook in the first 3 seconds: spoken line + 5-7 word on-screen text + a punch.
- A visual change every 3-5 seconds (cut, zoom, kinetic text, or b-roll).
- B-roll 15-35% of the runtime, 2-10 s per scene, always showing what is being said.
- Put the strongest moment (reveal) near 35-50% of the runtime as a re-hook; the music drop lands there.
- End on the CTA question and stop; no "thanks for watching".
